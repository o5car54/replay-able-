## Sending tips
- Send in small batches (20-30/day) from Gmail, not one big blast — protects deliverability.
- Space follow-ups 3-4 days apart; stop after 2-3 touches.
- Always honour "STOP" immediately.

## Compliance (UK)
- Limited companies: cold email allowed with a clear opt-out (the STOP line).
- Sole traders / ordinary partnerships: treated like individuals — need consent,
  so be selective. Honour every opt-out. Keeps you right with GDPR/PECR.
