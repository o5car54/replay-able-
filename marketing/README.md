# Replayable — sales & outreach kit

Your toolkit for getting the first Replayable customers.

## Files
- **`prospects-birmingham.csv`** — a starter list of ~50 real Birmingham trades
  (plumbers, electricians, roofers, builders, locksmiths, gas engineers,
  plasterers, painters/decorators, landscapers). Open it in Excel/Google Sheets.
- **`outreach-email.md`** — the cold email + follow-up to send them.

## How to use the list
1. Open `prospects-birmingham.csv` in Google Sheets (File → Import).
2. **Verify each contact before sending.** Phone numbers came from search results
   and may have small errors — the business's own **website is the source of truth**.
   Click the Website link, grab the real **email** from their Contact page, and
   confirm the phone.
3. Work top-down by **Fit**:
   - **High** = small operators / sole traders (mobile numbers, personal names).
     These miss the most calls and are your best-fit customers — start here.
   - **Med / Low** = bigger firms that may already run answering services.
4. Send Email 1 (from `outreach-email.md`), personalised. Log the date in the
   `Date contacted` column and update `Status` (e.g. Contacted → Replied → Demo → Won).
5. Send Email 2 to non-repliers after 3-4 days.

## Expanding the list
The starter list is a sample, not every trade in Birmingham. To grow it:
- Search **Google Maps** for "[trade] near Birmingham" and work through the pins —
  each listing shows phone, website, and often email.
- Browse directories: **Checkatrade, MyBuilder, TrustATrader, Yell, 192.com,
  Which? Trusted Traders**. Filter by Birmingham + trade.
- Add rows to the CSV in the same format.
- Ask me any time to pull another batch (e.g. "20 more electricians in Solihull").

## Targeting tip
The ideal Replayable customer is a **one-person or small trade** who genuinely
can't answer while working — not a big company with a receptionist. Prioritise the
"High" fit rows and any listing that's clearly a mobile number / personal name.

## Compliance
See the compliance note in `outreach-email.md`. Short version: fine to email
limited companies with an opt-out; be selective with sole traders; always honour
"STOP".
