Go live with Twilio

Simulation mode texts are logged, not sent. To send real SMS:

  1. Create a Twilio account & buy a UK number.
  2. Put TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID & TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN in app/.env.
  3. Point the number's Voice webhook to /webhooks/voice and Messaging webhook to /webhooks/sms.
  4. Set each client's forward-to number in their settings.
  5. Restart — the badge turns LIVE.
